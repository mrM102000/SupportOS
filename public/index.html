The Problem

Your Support Team Is Stuck
in a Broken Loop

When support volume grows, the answer shouldn't be "hire more people." The fix is removing work that should never reach a human.

🐌

Slow First Response

Customers wait hours. Some churn quietly. Some leave a review. Either way, you lose revenue you never see tracked.

🔁

Repetitive Ticket Work

60% of tickets are the same 20 questions. Your best agents spend their day copy-pasting answers they've written hundreds of times.

🔀

Wrong Routing

A billing issue hits the tech team. A refund bounces twice. Every misroute costs 8–15 minutes of re-reading context.

🧠

No Institutional Memory

Every call ends with inconsistent notes. The next agent starts from zero. Patterns stay invisible until they become a crisis.

What We Install

An Intelligent Layer Between
Message In and Human Responds

We don't replace your team or rebuild your stack. We install automation on top of what you already have.

01

AI Triage — Auto-tag and Categorize

Every incoming ticket is instantly classified by type, urgency, and customer tier — before any human touches it.

02

Smart Routing — Right Agent, First Time

Tickets go to the right team automatically. Eliminates misroutes and re-assignments entirely.

03

Auto-Response — Handle Easy Tickets Instantly

The top 10–15 ticket types get handled automatically. Customers get answers in seconds, not hours.

04

Agent-Assist — AI Inside Every Live Conversation

When a complex ticket reaches a human, AI drafts a reply inside Zendesk or Intercom. Agents send faster with more confidence.

05

Call & Chat Summarization → CRM Sync

Every conversation generates auto-formatted notes that sync directly into your CRM. Full context, every time.
